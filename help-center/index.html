Objective: This guide aims to minimize downtime and enhance your understanding of the Aquator Marine system by offering step-by-step troubleshooting methods, tips for using our support tools, and advice for preventing common issues.

#### Section 1: Common Issues and Solutions

1.1 Account and Login Problems

  • Issue: Difficulty logging in or forgotten passwords.
  • Solution: Use the 'Forgot Password?' link on the login page to reset your password. Follow the prompts to receive an email with instructions.

1.2 Setting up your User/Yacht Settings

  • Issue: Users may not have the correct access level to view or edit certain settings. A new user cannot see key information or perform necessary actions
  • Solution: Ensure that the correct role (Captain, crew, or Manager) is assigned. Adjust permissions in the User Management section under settings. If needed, contact an administrator to grant access.

1.3 Missing or Incorrect Yacht Information

  • Issue: Required fields (e.g., yacht name, IMO number, flag state) are left blank or entered incorrectly. Yacht data does not match the official registry.
  • Solution: Double-check yacht details with official documents before entering

1.4 Users Unable to Access the Yacht Profile

  • Issue: A user logs in but does not see the assigned yacht(s). Access to certain yacht settings is restricted.
  • Solution: Confirm that the user is assigned to the correct yacht(s) under User Management. Refresh the page or log out and back in to update changes. If still unresolved, check team assignments and ensure the user has been linked to the yacht.

  1. Navigate to Admin Console → Users → Roles.

  1. Navigate to Admin Console → Users → Roles.

  3. Roles with assigned users cannot be deleted directly — you'll be prompted to reassign those users to a different role first.

FAQs

Common questions, answered.

The 20 most-asked questions about the platform, in one expandable list.

What is Aquator Marine and who is it designed for?

Aquator Marine is an all-in-one yacht management platform designed for owners, captains, managers, and crew to centralise operations such as finance, accounting, maintenance, compliance, crew, and charter management.

Can Aquator Marine be used for both private and charter yachts?

Yes. The platform is built to support both private and charter yachts, with modules that can be enabled or disabled depending on your operational needs.

Is Aquator Marine suitable for single yachts and full fleets?

Absolutely. Aquator Marine scales easily from a single vessel to large fleets, making it ideal for owners, management companies, and family offices.

How long does onboarding and setup usually take?

Setup time depends on the size of the vessel and the amount of historical data being uploaded. Most yachts are up and running within a few days, with full implementation typically completed within a few weeks.

Do you assist with data migration from our existing systems?

Yes. Our team supports data migration from spreadsheets, legacy software, and other platforms to ensure a smooth transition.

Can different users have different access levels?

Yes. Aquator Marine offers role-based permissions so owners, captains, crew, accountants, and managers only see what is relevant to them.

Is the platform cloud-based?

Yes. Aquator Marine is fully cloud-based, meaning it can be accessed securely from anywhere with an internet connection.

Can Aquator Marine be accessed on mobile and tablet devices?

Yes. The platform is optimised for desktop, tablet, and mobile use, making it easy for crew to update information onboard or on the go.

How does Aquator Marine handle financial management?

The finance module covers budgets, expenses, approvals, reporting, and integration with accounting workflows, giving full transparency and control over yacht finances.

Does Aquator Marine support accounting and bookkeeping?

Yes. The accounting module includes chart of accounts, reconciliations, reporting, and export options to work seamlessly with external accountants.

Can we manage planned maintenance and inventory in the system?

Yes. The PMS module allows you to track planned and reactive maintenance, spare parts, inventory levels, and service history.

How does Aquator Marine support ISM and compliance?

Aquator Marine includes tools for ISM documentation, drill matrices, certificates, and compliance tracking to help ensure regulatory requirements are met.

Can charter data and charter history be stored in the platform?

Yes. Charter management features allow you to store historical charter data, manage current charters, and keep all related documentation in one place.

Is the platform available in multiple languages?

Yes. Aquator Marine supports multiple languages and can be fully translated to suit international teams and clients.

How secure is our data on Aquator Marine?

Data security is a top priority. The platform uses secure cloud infrastructure, encryption, and regular backups to protect your information.

Can Aquator Marine integrate with other tools or software?

Yes. Aquator Marine supports integrations and data exports to ensure it fits seamlessly into your existing workflows.

What kind of support is available if we need help?

Our support team is available to assist with onboarding, training, and day-to-day questions, ensuring you get the most out of the platform.

Can we customise the platform to suit our operations?

Yes. Modules, workflows, and reporting can be tailored to match how your yacht or company operates.

How often is the platform updated?

Aquator Marine is continuously improved with regular updates, new features, and enhancements based on user feedback.

How do we get started with Aquator Marine?

Simply book a demo with our team. We’ll walk you through the platform, understand your requirements, and guide you through the onboarding process.
